Laboratory of Quantum Sensing and Spectroscopy

Quantum measurements for materials and devices.

Research synopsis

We develop nanoscale quantum sensing and advanced optical spectroscopy to investigate electrodynamics and light–matter interactions in emerging materials. By integrating quantum measurement with advanced spectroscopic techniques, we aim to uncover microscopic physical mechanisms and enable the development of next-generation quantum, electronic, and photonic technologies.

Graduate students

We recruit new graduate students every year. Prospective students interested in joining our group should apply to the PhD or MS program in the Department of Mechanical Engineering. Applicants should hold an undergraduate degree in engineering, physics, optics, materials science, or a related field. We particularly welcome self-motivated, curious, and collaborative candidates with a strong interest in quantum science, quantum sensing, and optical experiments. Experience in optics, experimental physics, programming, electronics, or instrumentation is preferred.
