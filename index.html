Laboratory of Quantum Sensing and Spectroscopy

Quantum measurements for materials and devices.

Research synopsis

We develop nanoscale quantum sensing and advanced optical spectroscopy to investigate electrodynamics and light–matter interactions in emerging materials. By integrating quantum measurement with advanced spectroscopic techniques, we aim to uncover microscopic physical mechanisms and enable the development of next-generation quantum, electronic, and photonic technologies.
