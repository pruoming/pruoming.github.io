# Group Website: Main Features

## Purpose

Create a clear, professional, and responsive website that introduces the research group, explains its work, shares public outputs, and supports recruitment without publishing personal information.

## Core pages

- **Home** — short group introduction, research highlights, a representative image, recent updates, and links to key pages.
- **Research** — concise descriptions of the group’s main research areas, supported by diagrams or project images.
- **People** — team structure shown by role or category. Use role titles, group photos, or approved public profiles instead of private personal details.
- **Publications** — searchable or year-based list with titles, venues, years, and links to DOI, publisher, or preprint pages.
- **News** — dated announcements for publications, awards, events, projects, and open positions.
- **Gallery** — approved images of research, equipment, events, and group activities with descriptive alt text.
- **Join Us** — available roles, expected backgrounds, application steps, and a shared institutional contact channel.

## Shared website features

- Consistent header, navigation, footer, colors, typography, and page spacing.
- Mobile-friendly navigation and layouts for phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Reusable cards, grids, buttons, and content sections.
- Publication filtering by year or category.
- Accessible semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and useful image alt text.
- Fast-loading optimized images and minimal JavaScript.
- Search-engine metadata, social-sharing previews, sitemap, and clear page titles.
- Simple content structure so news, publications, members, and images are easy to update.

## Privacy rules

- Do not publish personal email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, birth dates, private schedules, identity numbers, or personal social-media accounts.
- Do not include personal biographies, CV details, portraits, or names unless each person has explicitly approved publication.
- Use a shared group or institutional email address for enquiries.
- Use only approved photographs; remove embedded image metadata before publishing when appropriate.
- Avoid showing badges, screens, documents, room access details, or sensitive equipment information in photographs.
- Add a short privacy notice if the site collects enquiries, analytics, cookies, or application files.
- Keep application documents out of the public website; send applicants to an approved secure submission system.

## Content placeholders

Use neutral placeholders until public content is approved:

- Group name and short mission statement
- Institution or department
- Research-area summaries
- Public publication links
- Shared contact address
- Approved group and research images
- Open-position descriptions

## Recommended first release

1. Launch Home, Research, Publications, and Join Us pages.
2. Add shared navigation, responsive styling, accessibility checks, and metadata.
3. Publish only reviewed content and approved images.
4. Add People, News, and Gallery pages when maintainers have a reliable update process.
5. Test links, mobile layouts, page speed, and privacy compliance before enabling search-engine indexing.

## Success criteria

- Visitors can understand the group’s purpose and research within one minute.
- Publications and opportunities are easy to find in two clicks or fewer.
- The site works well on mobile and with keyboard navigation.
- No unapproved personal information appears in page content, metadata, image files, or downloadable documents.
- A designated maintainer can update routine content without redesigning the site.
