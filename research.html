Research

Quantum Microscope

We develop quantum microscopes based on single-spin sensors to visualize electronic and magnetic phenomena at the nanoscale. By combining scanning quantum sensing with optical spectroscopy and electrical transport measurements, we probe local magnetism, superconductivity, current flow, and nonequilibrium electron dynamics with high spatial resolution and sensitivity. Our goal is to uncover microscopic processes inaccessible to conventional measurements and establish quantum microscopy as a powerful platform for investigating emerging quantum materials and devices.

Optoelectronic Devices

We develop optoelectronic devices based on low-dimensional and emerging materials for future electronic technologies. Through heterogeneous integration of different material systems, we explore new physical degrees of freedom and engineer enhanced device functionalities. Our aim is to harness these physical mechanisms to realize functional platforms for next-generation electronic, photonic, and quantum technologies.

Novel Quantum Platforms

We explore novel quantum platforms based on emerging materials, spin defects, and hybrid quantum systems to create new capabilities for sensing, information processing, and quantum control. Our research focuses on identifying and engineering quantum states with favorable optical, spin, and coherence properties, while developing material and device architectures that enhance their performance. These efforts aim to establish scalable quantum platforms for future sensing, communication, and information technologies.

Theories

We develop theoretical frameworks for spin–mechanical coupling and hybrid quantum devices, with a focus on harnessing interactions between quantum spins, mechanical motion, and phonons to enhance sensing performance.
